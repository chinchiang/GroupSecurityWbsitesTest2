# Content governance

## Content states

Draft → technical review → classification/privacy review → owner approval → publish → periodic review → expire/archive.

Public PSIRT content uses maker-checker approval with PSIRT and Product Owner at minimum, adding Legal, Privacy, or Customer Team when required. The internal portal may link to approved public content but cannot publish internal case details to the public origin.

Every governed content item needs owner, scope, source, classification, jurisdiction, effective/review/expiry dates, version/hash, approvers, limitations, and rollback target. Expired evidence or policy is visibly stale and excluded from valid-coverage calculations.

AI-generated summaries are drafts. They must cite authorized, current sources; prompt-injection content is treated as untrusted; high-risk actions require human approval.
